# Portfolio V2 Vision

## Product Statement

Portfolio V2 is an interactive engineering proof system for Blake Schmitt. Its purpose is to help hiring managers, engineering leaders, founders, and potential clients quickly understand the problems he can solve and then inspect credible evidence of how he solves them.

## Core Narrative

I turn messy real world problems into trustworthy software systems.

The site should repeatedly prove four themes:

1. Systems thinking.
2. Product and business judgment.
3. Automation and applied AI used where they create practical leverage.
4. Engineering discipline through architecture, testing, observability, accessibility, and documented tradeoffs.

## Primary Audiences

1. Recruiters who need rapid role fit and credibility signals.
2. Engineering managers who want evidence of technical depth and decision quality.
3. Founders and small businesses who need someone able to understand operational pain and build useful systems.
4. Technical peers who may inspect repositories and implementation details.

## Desired First Minute Experience

A visitor should understand:

1. What Blake is professionally.
2. What kind of problems he solves.
3. Which two or three projects provide the strongest evidence.
4. What makes his work different from tutorial portfolios.
5. Where to inspect code, architecture, case studies, or demos.
6. How to contact him.

## Experience Direction

The design should feel technically sophisticated, intentional, alive, and personal without becoming a visual effects demo. Motion and interaction should explain systems, reveal evidence, or improve orientation.

The portfolio itself should be a flagship engineering artifact, not merely a wrapper around other projects.
