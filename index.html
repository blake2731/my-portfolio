Verified open source engineering

An upstream correctness fix merged into NVIDIA cuDF.

In November 2026, my contribution to NVIDIA's GPU DataFrame library was merged. It fixes null propagation in nullable Boolean AND and OR operations, where expressions such as True | <NA> and False & <NA> previously produced the wrong result.

The change reuses libcudf's existing null-aware logical operators and includes regression tests for truth tables, reflected and scalar operations, and the original DataFrame filtering failure.

SmallBizzWizz | Current product case study

From a public business website to concrete, testable repair guidance.

SmallBizzWizz is a public website scanner for local service businesses. It examines a constrained public conversion path for observable contact, booking, trust, measurement, and technical signals, then turns findings into actionable repair tickets. It does not infer private analytics or promise a revenue increase.

See live scanner Explore source

Type
Public website analysis tool
Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript
Focus
URL safety, deterministic scanning, validation
Engineering approach

Bounded inspection and practical results

The scanner normalizes and checks a submitted URL, rejects internal network targets, limits redirects, time, and fetch size, and reviews a small number of likely contact or booking pages. It reports observable evidence rather than guessing at a business's conversion rate.

How it is validated

Reproducible synthetic proof

Public before and after fixtures exercise the scanner against known page differences. The proof page shows what was detected, while the methodology documents important blind spots such as private analytics and JavaScript only behavior.
